Explicit coordination

A shared model of how GoreeCloud fits together.

Mesh replaces hidden coupling with registered capabilities and explicit relationships. Discovery does not grant authorization, and coordination does not become authority transfer.

01

Registry

Records approved service identities, kinds, versions, capabilities, dependencies, labels, state, and conformance context.

02

Graph

Models direct relationships and computes dependency context so impact can be explained rather than guessed.

03

Policy

Fails closed when actors, targets, capabilities, availability, or enabled relationships do not satisfy the registered contract.

04

Events

Moves bounded relationship and lifecycle events while keeping application payload ownership with the source system.

Authority boundaries

Connect the systems. Do not collapse them.

Mesh coordinates across first-party systems while their specialized responsibilities stay where they belong.

Identity

Authentication, authorization, accounts, devices, credentials, sessions, and delegated authority remain Identity responsibilities.

Wardveil Security

Protection, trust, verification, detection, response, and security evidence remain security responsibilities.

Privacy Shield

Consent, minimization, governance, retention boundaries, and user control remain privacy responsibilities.

Everkeep

Backup, recovery, preservation, portability, succession, and continuity remain resilience responsibilities.

Core invariantauthority_transfer = false

Relationship discovery and coordination are never an authentication bypass, a transport shortcut, or permission to mutate another system.
